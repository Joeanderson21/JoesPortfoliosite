About me

I spent years leading teams and training staff in early childhood education. I taught myself cloud engineering and now build on AWS. My projects cover static sites, ETL workloads, event-driven and serverless architectures, and cost optimization.

Early childhood education

Before cloud, I worked in early childhood education as a Lead Teacher at the Denver JCC and as Director of Early Childhood Education at The Goddard School. I kept classrooms safe and licensed under Colorado standards, trained staff, and documented children's learning for families through blog posts, Brightwheel messages and classroom displays.

What carries over to cloud work

  • Documentation. I write things down so other people can follow them, whether that is a learning story for parents or a runbook.
  • Safety and standards. Working to state licensing rules made me careful about checklists, access and safe defaults, which is the same mindset as IAM and security guardrails.
  • Training people. I onboarded staff on tools like multi-factor authentication and SaaS apps, and I explain technical things plainly.
  • Staying calm under pressure. De-escalation and clear communication help when something breaks.
