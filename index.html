Early childhood leadership

Today I am the Family Enrollment Director for The Learning Experience centers in Thornton, Orchard and Lafayette, Colorado. Before that I directed five programs in Colorado and New York, from opening a new school in Boulder to supporting four Manhattan Goddard schools. I have also worked as a compliance manager across five schools and as a teacher.

What I do well

  • Licensing and inspection readiness. I have moved a center into stronger compliance after inspection issues, run quality-assurance inspections, and prepared schools for NYC DOHMH inspections.
  • Training and developing staff. I have recruited, trained and evaluated teams, mentored teachers until two became directors, and presented at the 2023 Early Childhood Colorado Leadership Conference.
  • Enrollment growth. At The Learning Experience I drove 85 new enrollments across two centers in my first two quarters. At Goddard Louisville I raised full-time-equivalent enrollment by more than 30.
  • Steady leadership in a crisis. I led a school through COVID-19, closing for only three weeks, with no reported cases through August 2020.
